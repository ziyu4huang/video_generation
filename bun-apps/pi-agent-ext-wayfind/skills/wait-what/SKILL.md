# Wait — What?

Stop. That last message did not land — re-pitch it.

Give a little bit of context, talk in **ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English** (short sentences, one idea per sentence, the approved words only), and use the **ubiquitous language** from `CONTEXT.md` — the project's own settled terms, not your synonyms for them. If `CONTEXT.md` doesn't exist yet, say the plain thing in the fewest correct words.

Say what you were doing, where you are now, and what you're about to do next — nothing else. Then wait.
