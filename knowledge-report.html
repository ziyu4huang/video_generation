Top 5 Prompt Strategies

1. Use LoRA-specific trigger words at the start of prompts

Example: slider, bodyweight, full body shot of a woman... or anatomyfix, full body shot of a woman...

Trigger words activate LoRA behavior early, ensuring the model applies specialized training from the first step. This consistently yields perfect prompt_adherence (10/10) and overall scores (10/10).

2. Specify anatomical details explicitly

Example: ...with five visible fingers on each hand, visible joint definition in wrists and elbows, torso twisted with clear ribcage and hip alignment

Detailed anatomical instructions eliminate common failures like missing fingers or distorted joints. Prompts with such specificity scored 10/10 on prompt_adherence and detail.

3. Combine lighting, texture, and sharpness keywords

Example: natural afternoon side lighting casting long shadows, photorealistic, detailed hands and face, ultra sharp focus

This triad (lighting + texture + sharpness) consistently produces high scores in detail (9–10), sharpness (9–10), and overall (9–10) across both pipelines.

4. Use descriptive, scene-based prompts for zimage

Example: photorealistic portrait of a young woman, sharp eyes with detailed irises, natural skin texture with fine pores, soft studio lighting, bokeh background, high detail, ultra sharp focus

zimage excels with natural language descriptions including multiple sensory details, yielding consistent 9/10 scores across all dimensions.

5. Balance LoRA scale for optimal results

LoRA scale 0.5–0.8 for bodyweight preserves natural variation; scale 1.0–1.2 for anatomy ensures precise joint and muscle rendering. Both ranges produce 10/10 scores.

Prompt Patterns That Correlate With High Scores

  • Trigger word + scene + anatomical detail + lighting + quality keywords → 10/10 overall, 10/10 prompt_adherence
  • "photorealistic" + "ultra sharp focus" + specific texture keywords → 9–10/10 detail and sharpness
  • "soft studio lighting" + "bokeh background" → 9/10 composition and artifacts
  • Explicit finger/joint descriptions → eliminates common anatomy failures
  • "high dynamic range" + "dramatic sky" → 9–10/10 composition for landscapes
